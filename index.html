How games are rated

Each game gets a rating from 0 to 10 by comparing you with all 10 players in that game, not with fixed numbers, so a 40% kill participation means something different in a stomp than in a close game. Stats are weighted by role: supports mostly on kill participation and vision, junglers on kill participation and impact, carries on damage, gold and CS, top laners also on damage absorbed. Your gold lead over your direct lane opponent counts too, and winning gives a small bonus. Ratings are calibrated so every role averages 5.0. The best rating in the lobby is the MVP; the lowest is "Worst in lobby".

Game of the day is the highest-rated game since midnight; inter of the day is the lowest, even if that game was won.

Standings

Ranks are each player's current Solo/Duo rank. Roles are estimated (Riot doesn't share them during a game) from summoner spells and how often each champion is played in each role; the estimate is right for about 95% of players. "Against these enemy champions" counts your friend's race games where that champion was on the other team.

Solid lines are real LP, checked every minute since Oct 4. Dashed lines are rebuilt from match results (about ±20 LP per game, adjusted to end on the real LP) because Riot doesn't keep past LP. Values marked ≈ are estimates.

Click a column to sort, click a champion for who plays it and their best game. Rating is the average lobby rating (0–10) on that champion; sorting by rating or win rate lists champions with 2+ games first.
